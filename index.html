Clear Clinical Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, structured answers to questions regarding pain mitigation, out-of-pocket costs, same-day crowns, insurance, and emergency dental protocols.

help Is modern dental surgery truly pain-free?

Yes. Incisors Med adheres to multi-modal sedation and local computer-assisted anesthesia (The Wand™). Patients experiencing dental phobia or surgical extraction can select Twilight IV Sedation, falling into a deeply relaxing sleep with zero memory of the procedure.

help How much do dental implants cost, and is financing available?

Single-tooth implant packages start with itemized upfront quotes, typically ranging from $1,900 to $3,800 depending on bone density graft needs. We partner with CareCredit and Proceed Finance to provide 0% APR monthly installments starting from $89/month.

help Can I receive a permanent crown on the exact same day?

Yes. With our in-house CEREC CAD/CAM milling units, we capture optical scans, design your anatomy in 3D, and mill high-strength lithium disilicate ceramic crowns in approximately 45 minutes, eliminating temporary crowns and return appointments.

help What qualifies as an emergency dental visit, and are you open 24/7?

Severe throbbing pain, facial swelling, avulsed (knocked out) adult teeth, uncontrollable bleeding, or broken bridge work qualify as immediate emergencies. Our 24/7 hotline directs on-call surgical triage with operatory access within 2 hours.

help How do porcelain veneers differ from composite bonding?

Porcelain veneers are custom ceramic shells with an average lifespan of 15–20 years, highly stain-resistant, and mimicking natural tooth enamel reflections. Composite bonding is applied resin sculpted directly chairside, suited for minor chips and lasting 4–7 years.

help Do you accept out-of-state or out-of-network dental plans?

Yes. Our concierge patient finance team files all primary and secondary claims directly on your behalf, maximizing standard out-of-network reimbursements and providing clear estimations prior to any treatment step.
